|---|---|---|---|

| **GAP-SEC-02** | **`organization-service` Open Write/PII Endpoints** | `services/organization-service/.../SecurityConfig.java:60-68` (`.anyRequest().permitAll()`) | Authenticate everything except explicit health/docs endpoints; restrict branch, service-area, and merchant-admin queries/writes by role. |
| **GAP-SEC-03** | **Cross-Branch IDOR & `X-Branch-Id` Header Spoofing** | `identity/.../JwtService.java:56-65`; `DispatchUserPrincipal.java:31-42`; `DispatchJwtAuthenticationFilter.java:80-89`; `OrderService.java:379-394,456-475,614-616` | Associate users with `branchId` / `organizationId` in Identity Service, embed claims in JWT, remove client `X-Branch-Id` override, fail closed when branch-scoped staff lack `branchId`, and enforce branch checks in Order, Dispatch, and Notification services. |
| **GAP-SEC-04** | **Self-Service Dropoff, Courier Cancellation & Cross-Courier Status Tampering** | `OrderService.java:456-495,572-608,617-622` (`recordBranchDropoff` & `cancelOrder` reuse `enforceOrderReadAccess`) | Restrict `recordBranchDropoff` to authorized branch/dispatch staff (`DISPATCHER`/`BRANCH_STAFF`/`ADMIN`), restrict `cancelOrder` to customer/merchant owner or admin/dispatcher, and restrict `COURIER` status updates to `order.assignedCourierId`. |
| **GAP-SEC-05** | **Unauthenticated `/actuator/**` & `/test-items/**` + Permissive S2S Token** | `SecurityConfig.java` across services; `DispatchJwtService.java:98-108` (`ADMIN` embedded on all S2S calls) | Lock down `/test-items/**` and `/actuator/**` (allow only `/actuator/health` and `/health` anonymously); issue narrow `INTERNAL_SERVICE` principal claims with `iss` checks instead of `"ADMIN"`. |
| **GAP-SEC-06** | **CSRF Disabled on Cookie-Only Identity Auth + Missing `iss` Validation** | `identity/.../JwtAuthenticationFilter.java:43`; `identity/.../SecurityConfig.java:53`; `OrganizationJwtService`, `OrderJwtService`, `DispatchJwtService`, `NotificationJwtService` | Support Bearer or enforce CSRF/double-submit + `SameSite` + `Secure` cookies uniformly, and verify `iss = "swiftdrop-identity-service"` in all resource services. |
| **GAP-SEC-07** | **Public Tracking PII Leakage** | `OrderService.java:442-443`; `DispatchService.java:904-911,1065-1068,1197-1200`; `NotificationController.java:42-47` | Sanitize public `TrackingEvent` descriptions/locations to city/branch milestones only (no street addresses or full recipient names), and omit/mask `recipientName`/`orderId`/`correlationId` in `/notifications/tracking/{trackingNumber}`. |
| **GAP-SEC-08** | **Live Credentials Committed to VCS** | `infrastructure/dev/.env.example:9-70`; `services/identity-service/src/main/resources/application.yml:8-10,67,72` | Rotate exposed Neon PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ, Google OAuth, and JWT secrets immediately and replace defaults with non-secret local placeholders. |
