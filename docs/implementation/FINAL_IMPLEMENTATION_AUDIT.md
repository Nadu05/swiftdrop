# SwiftDrop – Final Production-Readiness & Business Flow Audit

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Authoritative Business Baseline:** [SwiftDrop_Business_Process.md](file:///C:/Users/naduk/.gemini/antigravity/worktrees/EAD/identity_service_auth_foundation/agents/SwiftDrop_Business_Process.md), [SwiftDrop_Finalized_Business_Decisions.md](file:///C:/Users/naduk/.gemini/antigravity/worktrees/EAD/identity_service_auth_foundation/agents/SwiftDrop_Finalized_Business_Decisions.md) (`BD-01`–`BD-08`, `UD-01`–`UD-24`)  
**Scope:** Full read-only forensic inspection of `api-gateway`, `services/{identity,organization,order,dispatch,notification}-service`, `frontend`, `infrastructure/{dev,local}`, and automated test suites. No architectural edits were made during this audit.

> **Executive Verdict:** SwiftDrop has working core implementations across authentication, branch/service-area setup, order quoting/booking, tracking timelines, dispatch assignment, transfer manifests, proof-of-delivery capture, and notification persistence, with **270 backend Maven tests** and **52 Next.js routes** compiling cleanly. However, the system is **not production-ready**. Source-level inspection exposed **critical authentication/authorization bypasses**, **inter-service split-brain**, **broken DTO/enum contracts**, **mocked payment/email/verification boundaries**, **Flyway-vs-entity drift masked by H2 tests**, and **Docker configuration omissions** that will fail container startup unless remediated.

## 2. Detailed Defect Analysis Across the 7 Audit Dimensions

### 2.1 Incorrect Business Rules
1. **Pickup failure conflated with delivery failure (`SwiftDrop_Business_Process.md §6.1`):** `PickupTaskStatus` (`services/dispatch-service/.../PickupTaskStatus.java:3-9`) has no failed status; calling `POST /fail` on a pickup assignment throws 422 `NOT_A_DELIVERY_JOB` (`DispatchService.java:1114-1120`), violating the requirement to record failed pickups separately.
2. **Physical collection & origin-branch intake collapsed (`Step 5` vs `Step 6`):** Calling `markPickedUp` (`DispatchService.java:884-952`) immediately sets both `PICKED_UP` and `AT_ORIGIN_BRANCH` and emits `ParcelPickedUp` + `ParcelReceivedAtBranch` without branch staff physical handover (`branchConfirmedByEmployeeId` remains `null`).
3. **Rescheduled 4th attempt & branch hold break business flow (`BD-07`):** `HOLD_AT_BRANCH` sets `DeliveryJob.status = UNASSIGNED` without incrementing `maxAttempts`, permanently locking assignment at `attemptCount >= maxAttempts` (`DispatchService.java:1341-1344, 565-567`). `RESCHEDULE_DELIVERY` immediately sends `"OUT_FOR_DELIVERY"` to Order Service before courier assignment (`1328-1340`), breaking subsequent pickup sync.
4. **Return-to-sender shortcuts & blocked multi-hop transfers (`§6.5`):** `TransferManifestService.resolveEligibleParcelCandidate` (`854-867`) rejects `RETURN_TO_SENDER` orders; `ReturnTaskService.confirmReturnToSender` (`558-590`) permits closing directly from `PENDING_ASSIGNMENT`/`COURIER_ASSIGNED` without origin-branch arrival.
5. **Booking allows unapproved merchants & bypasses branch coverage (`Step 1B`, `Step 2`):** `OrderService.resolveOrderSourceType` (`718-732`) accepts `MERCHANT` orders from non-merchant accounts without querying `organization-service` approval; `AddressCoordinateResolver.java:21-67` defaults unknown addresses to Colombo coordinates instead of failing uncovered locations (`UD-05`).
6. **Manifest completion ignores open exceptions & unverified scans:** `TransferManifestService.classifyManifestParcels` (`953-960`) defaults omitted parcel lists to `RECEIVED` on `POST /receive`, while `completeManifest` (`612-645`) succeeds even when `MISSING_PARCEL`/`DAMAGED_PARCEL` exceptions remain `OPEN`.
7. **COD payment automatically marked collected without cash capture (`§8.1`, `14.2`):** `OrderLifecycleService.java:226-228` flips order `paymentStatus` to `COD_COLLECTED` upon `DELIVERED` without receiving or verifying any collected amount from `DispatchService`, while leaving `PaymentReference` stuck at `COD_PENDING_COLLECTION`.
8. **Single-recipient notification logic misses Section 9 recipients:** `NotificationService.buildOrderNotification` (`448-485`) creates one record per event favoring `customerEmail`, so Couriers never receive `Pickup assigned` notifications and `OrderCreated` is skipped unless `paymentStatus == PENDING_PAYMENT`.

### 2.2 Inconsistent Statuses & Contract Drift
- **Frontend vs Dispatch enum:** `frontend/src/api/services/dispatch.api.ts:9` defines `DispatchPickupMethod = 'COURIER_PICKUP' | 'BRANCH_DROP_OFF'`, whereas backend `PickupMethod.java:5` and `order-service` use `BRANCH_DROPOFF`. `dispatcher/dashboard/page.tsx:92` and `deliveries/page.tsx:281` never match backend drop-off tasks.
- **Dispatch `DeliveryJobDto` vs entity/frontend:** `recordFailedDelivery` (`DispatchService.java:1167,1221,2176`) persists `DeliveryJobStatus.PENDING_DISPATCHER_DECISION` but returns `AssignmentStatus.FAILED` (`"FAILED"`) in the DTO response (which `DispatchEndToEndAndExceptionTest.java:342-346` asserts). Meanwhile frontend `dispatch.api.ts:41,135,146-159,178-184` and mappers read `createdAt`, `dropoffCity`, `parcelInfo.category`, `parcel*.notes`, and `attempts[].status`, whereas backend `DeliveryJobDto.java:24-52,292-300` serializes `orderDate`, `city`, `parcelInfo.packageType`/`handlingNotes`, and `attempts[].outcome`.
- **Courier profile DTO drift:** Backend `CourierProfileDto.AssignedOrderItemDto` returns `destination`, while frontend `dispatch.api.ts:211-218` and `couriersApi.ts:52-59` read `dropoffCity` and `merchantName`.
- **State-machine naming & guard gaps:** `DispatchService` calls `markPickedUp` -> `IN_TRANSIT` / `AT_ORIGIN_BRANCH`, whereas `OrderLifecycleService.ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS` (`lines 56-68,83`) permits `PICKED_UP -> OUT_FOR_DELIVERY`, `AT_ORIGIN_BRANCH -> OUT_FOR_DELIVERY` without checking `order.isSameBranch()`, `OUT_FOR_DELIVERY -> PENDING_DISPATCHER_DECISION` with `failedAttemptCount == 0`, and `-> DELIVERED` via `PATCH /status` without POD proof.

### 2.3 Duplicate Responsibilities Across Microservices
1. **Courier identity & profiles (`identity-service` vs `dispatch-service`):** Dispatch stores independent `couriers` rows (6 seeded in V2) and synthesizes fake Colombo profiles in `DispatchService.resolveOrProvisionCurrentCourier` (`1545-1577`) instead of syncing from `identity-service` (`AdminService`).
2. **Duplicate parcel status coordination (`order-service` vs `dispatch-service`):** Dispatch drives order lifecycle via synchronous HTTP (`RestOrderServiceClient` -> `PATCH /orders/{id}/status` and `POST /orders/{id}/branch-dropoff`) alongside parallel RabbitMQ outbox events, while `order-service` has zero `@RabbitListener`s and an unused `order.events.queue`/DLQ.
3. **Synchronous status override & tracking duplication:** `DispatchService` duplicates Order Service's branch drop-off toggle and courier metadata onto `DeliveryJob`/`PickupTask` entities without transactional consistency, while `TransferManifestService.receiveManifest` creates `DeliveryJob`s directly and simultaneously triggers `OrderStatusChanged` back to `OrderEventListener`.

### 2.4 Missing Authorization Checks & API Security Vulnerabilities
1. **CRITICAL — Unauthenticated Header Authentication Bypass:** `OrderJwtAuthenticationFilter.java:43-60` and `NotificationJwtAuthenticationFilter.java:41-56` authenticate callers directly from `X-User-Id`, `X-User-Email`, `X-Organization-Id`, and `X-User-Roles` when a valid JWT is absent. `api-gateway` (`application.yml`, `CorrelationIdGlobalFilter.java`) neither validates JWTs nor strips client `X-*` headers.
2. **CRITICAL — `organization-service` Open Endpoints:** `services/organization-service/.../SecurityConfig.java:60-68` only guards `POST /merchants/applications/*/approve` and `.../reject`; `.anyRequest().permitAll()` leaves branch writes (`POST/PATCH /branches`), service area writes (`POST/PATCH /service-areas`), merchant listing/creation (`POST/GET /merchants/applications`), and `/actuator/**` publicly accessible without authentication.
3. **CRITICAL — Cross-Branch IDOR & `X-Branch-Id` Spoofing:** `identity-service` `JwtService.java:56-65` never embeds `branchId`; `DispatchUserPrincipal.java:39` (`if (branchId == null) return true`) grants unrestricted cross-branch access to every real Identity `DISPATCHER` token, and `DispatchJwtAuthenticationFilter.java:80-89` trusts client-supplied `X-Branch-Id`. In `order-service`, `listOrders`, `enforceOrderReadAccess`, and `updateOrderStatus` (`OrderService.java:379-394,456-475,614-616`) omit branch filtering and courier assignment ownership checks.
4. **CRITICAL — Unpaid Booking / Self-Dropoff / Admin Retry Abuse:**
   - `OrderService.recordBranchDropoff` (`477-495`) uses `enforceOrderReadAccess`, enabling an owning `CUSTOMER` or `MERCHANT` to self-mark parcels `AT_ORIGIN_BRANCH`.
   - `OrderService.cancelOrder` (`572-608`) uses `enforceOrderReadAccess`, allowing an assigned `COURIER` to cancel an order.
   - `DispatchService` synchronous pickup/job/assignment endpoints accept `PENDING_PAYMENT` `PREPAID` orders (`87-128,222-242,1652-1749`).
   - `frontend/src/app/organization/**` lacks `RoleGuard` and middleware coverage (`frontend/src/middleware.ts:4-14`).
5. **Cookie/CSRF, JWT Issuer & Secret Hygiene:** `identity-service` `JwtAuthenticationFilter.java:43` ignores `Authorization: Bearer` while all 5 services disable CSRF; downstream services do not enforce JWT `iss = "swiftdrop-identity-service"` and `DispatchJwtService.generateInternalServiceToken` (`98-108`) elevates S2S calls to `"ADMIN"`; real Neon DB, JWT, RabbitMQ, and Google Client secrets are committed in `application.yml` & `.env.example`.
6. **Public PII Exposure:** `DispatchService` embeds raw pickup/delivery street addresses and recipient names into `TrackingEvent.location`/`description` (`904-911,1065-1068,1197-1200`), which `OrderService.getPublicTracking` (`442-443`) exposes unredacted, and `NotificationController.getPublicTrackingNotifications` (`42-47`, `.permitAll()`) returns raw `recipientName`, `orderId`, and `correlationId`.

### 2.5 Unhandled Failure Scenarios
- `RestOrderServiceClient` (`86-89, 109-112`) catches and swallows all exceptions during status and branch drop-off notifications, producing silent split-brain between Dispatch and Order databases when `order-service` restarts or rejects a state transition.
- `PodEvidenceStorageService.persistDataUriToSecureFile` (`170-173`) catches IO errors, logs a warning, and persists a broken `pod-storage://...` reference despite zero bytes saved; `docker-compose.yml` also mounts no volume on `dispatch-service`.
- `OrderService.resolveOrderSourceType` + `cancelOrder`: Order cancellation does not cancel active Dispatch pickup/delivery assignments; `confirmPrepaidPayment` (`513-570`) lacks terminal/paid state guards; `/track` ETA says `"Within 24 hours"` even for `CANCELLED`/`RETURNED_TO_SENDER`.
- `DispatchService.resolveOrderSummaryForDropoff` (`1751-1787`) fabricates synthetic order data on `order-service` 5xx errors instead of failing or queueing cleanly.
- `NotificationService.retryFailedNotifications` (`235-265`) calls `NotificationAuditHelper.recordFailureInNewTx` (`20-38`) using the same `notificationCode`, throwing `DataIntegrityViolationException` (`UQ`) on repeat delivery failures and aborting the retry loop on the first failure; `@EnableScheduling` is absent from `NotificationServiceApplication`.
- Outbox schedulers (`OrganizationOutboxEventRepository`, `OrderOutboxEventRepository`, `DispatchOutboxEventRepository`) query `findTop50...` with no lock (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`), creating multi-instance duplicate publish hazards; max retry exhaustion ends in `FAILED` with no `DEAD` state.
- Inbox checks (`existsByEventIdAndConsumerGroup` -> processing -> `save`) in `OrderEventListener` and `NotificationService` lack atomic `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` / unique-violation recovery.

### 2.6 Data Consistency Issues
- **Entity vs Flyway DDL drift masked by H2 (`create-drop`):** `dispatch-service` and `notification-service` `TestItem.java` map `description` as `columnDefinition = "TEXT"` and nullable `updatedAt`, violating `V1` DDL (`VARCHAR(1000)` and `NOT NULL DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`); `identity-service` `V5` uses `TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE` for fields mapped as timezone-less `LocalDateTime`; `identity-service/pom.xml` mixes Flyway 9 (`flyway-core:9.22.3`) with `flyway-database-postgresql:10.15.0`.
- **Missing database referential & domain integrity:** `order`, `dispatch`, and `notification` Flyway schemas contain zero `CHECK` constraints; `dispatch` omits foreign keys from `delivery_exceptions.return_task_id -> return_tasks(id)` (`V3:77`) and multiple `courier_id` columns -> `couriers(id)` (`V2:68,122,194,215`, `V3:147`).
- **Stale cross-service / manifest counters:** `MARK_FOUND_RESUME` (`OperationalExceptionService.java:336-360`) fails to update `manifest.missingParcelCount` and `manifest.receivedParcelCount`; Dispatch assignment capacity leaks on `REASSIGNED` manifests/returns (`DispatchSupportService.java:219-247`); `DispatchService.reassignCourier` (`667-771`) does not update Order's cached courier metadata; `confirmPrepaidPayment` / `DELIVERED` leave `DeliveryOrder` and `PaymentReference` payment statuses out of sync for COD.
- **Non-atomic Order creation & missing quote lock:** `OrderService.createOrder` does not validate `PricingQuoteRepository` expiry or prevent duplicate bookings via idempotency keys.

### 2.7 Broken Frontend API Integrations
1. **Enum mismatch (`'BRANCH_DROP_OFF'` vs `'BRANCH_DROPOFF'`):** `frontend/src/api/services/dispatch.api.ts:9`, `dispatcher/dashboard/page.tsx:92`, and `deliveries/page.tsx:281` check `'BRANCH_DROP_OFF'`, failing to detect backend `'BRANCH_DROPOFF'` tasks.

4. **Live mock & hardcoded UI modules:** `/customers` (`CustomerSummarySection.tsx`), `/dispatcher/zones-pricing` (`zonesPricingApi.ts`), `(platform)/page.tsx` metric banner, `/tracking` static `<svg>` route map, `/dispatcher/{users,settings}`, `/demo-login`, and disabled `OrganizationSidebar` tabs remain mock/static.

## 3. Recommended Remediation Priority Order

1. **P0 — Security & Secrets (Immediate):** Remove `X-User-*` header fallback from `OrderJwtAuthenticationFilter` & `NotificationJwtAuthenticationFilter`; remove `X-Branch-Id` client override in `DispatchJwtAuthenticationFilter`; lock down `organization-service` `SecurityConfig` (remove `.anyRequest().permitAll()`, restrict `/actuator/**` & `/test-items/**`); add Gateway JWT validation/header stripping; introduce `branchId`/`organizationId`/`BRANCH_MANAGER`/`BRANCH_STAFF`/`HEAD_OFFICE_ADMIN` claims & checks; rotate and purge committed Neon/OAuth/RabbitMQ/JWT secrets; redact street addresses/names from public `TrackingEvent` & `NotificationController` tracking responses.
2. **P1 — Core Workflow & Data Consistency:** Block `PREPAID` (`PENDING_PAYMENT`) orders in synchronous Dispatch entry points; verify merchant approval + role in `OrderService`; eliminate `AddressCoordinateResolver` silent Colombo fallback; implement Pickup Failure distinct from Delivery Failure; fix `HOLD_AT_BRANCH`, `RESCHEDULE_DELIVERY` 4th-attempt sync, `REASSIGNED` workload leak, `MARK_FOUND_RESUME` manifest counts, and `NotificationAuditHelper` retry collision; align `TestItem`/Flyway DDL & `pom.xml` Flyway version.
3. **P2 — Contract Parity, Frontend & Docker Hardening:** Fix `'BRANCH_DROPOFF'` enum and `DeliveryJobDto`/`AssignedOrderItemDto` mappings; wire `/customer/profile` to `/users/me`; remove remaining `/customers` & `/dispatcher/zones-pricing` mocks or gate behind feature flags; wire Transfer Manifest/Return/Notification UIs and Merchant/Branch portals; fix `docker-compose.yml` (`frontend`, `JWT_SECRET` on all 5 services, `*_SERVICE_URL`, POD volume, Dockerfiles, scripts).
